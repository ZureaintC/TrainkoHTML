FAQ

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are billed through Apple App Store (StoreKit 2). You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime via iOS Settings → [Your Apple ID] → Subscriptions → Trainko, or via 'Manage Subscriptions' inside the App. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your billing cycle ends.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

No account is required. Trainko follows a strict local-first architecture. All your training records, gym setups, and profile metrics are stored securely on your iPhone. For Premium members who enable iCloud Sync, data is synced directly via Apple CloudKit in your personal private database — no developer server ever sees your data.

Can I turn off the local HTTP API service?

Yes. You can toggle the local API service on or off anytime in Settings → API Service. When turned off, the local HTTP server shuts down completely, preserving privacy and battery.

Which AI agents are supported?

Any AI Agent that can make HTTP requests — including desktop AI agents, custom automation workflows, and local scripts.
